Genius OBD – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Genius OBD ("the app", "we") is a car diagnostics app that reads data from your car through an OBD2 adapter. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes and how to delete it.

Summary

  • No sign-up. The app does not collect your location, contacts or account data.
  • Car data stays on your phone. It leaves the phone only when you act: send a report for analysis, request norms for your car, or save or share a records archive.
  • We do not sell data.

Data stored on your phone

Car data (trip records, trouble codes, sensor readings, trip summaries, car details, notes, received reports) is stored on your phone.

"Delete all" under Records in Settings deletes the selected car's trip records, raw logs, event log and voice notes. Records of other cars are deleted after you switch to each of them in My cars. Car details, the VIN, trip summaries, notes, survey answers and received reports stay until you clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the app.

When data leaves your phone

Data leaves the phone only when you act: when you send a report for analysis, request norms for your car, save or share a records archive.

About once a week the app also downloads updates of the norms database from our server. That request sends no data about you or your car.

Reports for analysis

A report for analysis goes to our server and to Anthropic (USA), the AI provider that writes the analysis. It does not contain the VIN, location, name, phone number or email.

The server keeps the package only until the analysis is finished. The finished report is kept for at least 30 days so the app can download it again, and after that for as long as we need it to check and improve the quality of the analysis.

Norms requests

A norms request sends the install ID and the car make, model, year and engine (no VIN). The server keeps it for 30 days after the answer, or 90 days if there is no answer, and deletes it with your reports when you email us.

To stop one person from flooding us with norms requests, the server also keeps a scrambled code made from your internet address, not the address itself, for one day.

Install ID and backup

The install ID is a random number created by the app. It is shown under Settings, Privacy. It is the only thing from the app saved in your Google backup, so purchased reports survive reinstalling the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and report packs are handled by Google Play. The server checks the purchase with Google and never sees your payment details. For packs bought without a subscription the server keeps how many reports are left and when they expire (one year after purchase).

Ads

The free version may show ads from Google AdMob, which uses the advertising ID. Car data and reports are never sent to the ad service.

Deleting your data

  • On the phone: use "Delete all" under Records in Settings, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app.
  • On the server: email namarobd2@gmail.com with the install ID shown under Settings, Privacy. We delete your reports and norms requests.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we update this page and the effective date above.
